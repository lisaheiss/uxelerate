Voguex Build Audit

Differences worth correcting between the new live build and the approved Figma experience. Four screens audited on the English build: Home, Results, Venue page, and the Hold checkout. Overall the build is faithful to the design's structure and flow — the gaps are concentrated in content, data binding, and trust-sensitive copy.

0Global issues

These appear on several screens; fixing them once clears most of the list.

Blocker

Design placeholder brackets shipped as literal text

In the Figma file, square brackets mark “replace with live data”. The build renders them verbatim across every screen: [2] available that day, ratings as ★ [0] and [4.8], [24] couples booked with Voguex this month, testimonial names [Daniela Molgora], and the checkout placeholder [Mariana] and [Diego].

Strip every bracket and bind the real value; where no real value exists yet (e.g. follower count was [XX.X mil] in design), hide the element rather than show the placeholder.

Blocker

Default search date is in the past

Hero, Results and the Venue page all come preloaded with Sat, 14 Nov 2026 — three days ago. A visitor lands on a search for a date that can no longer be booked. The design ships the date field empty (“Elige la fecha si ya la sabes” / “Choose a date if you know it”).

No preset date on the hero per design; date picker minimum = today; never persist a past date in the session.

Should fix

Two different discount promises

Home step 1 says “Up to 14% off on selected dates”; the design says up to 50% (“ofertas de hasta 50% en fechas seleccionadas”), and the build's own Results tip card also says “weekday offers up to -50%”. One of these is wrong.

Confirm the real maximum with the venue group and use the same figure everywhere (home step 1, results tip, price badges).

Should fix

Language mixing on the English build

The EN page renders Spanish review content in the testimonial cards, and venue descriptions carry untranslated fragments. The Spanish locale wasn't part of this audit's screenshots — it needs the same pass.

All visible content follows the active locale; run this same checklist on the ES build.

Polish

Trust-bar copy drifts between screens

Home: “+1,000 group weddings”. Venue page: “+1000 weddings by the group” (no separator, different phrasing). Design intent: weddings held by the venue group — “+1,000 weddings by the group”.

One phrasing, with the thousands separator, reused on both screens.

1Home

Blocker

Testimonials are raw venue reviews — including a damning one

“Couples who got married with Voguex” renders what look like scraped Google reviews of the venues: in Spanish on the EN page, without the design's five-star row, and the third card is a long, furious safety complaint, shown verbatim with raw <br> tags as text:

“UNA VERDADERA DECEPCIÓN Y EXPERIENCIA INSEGURA. No lo recomiendo… INTENTARON ABRIR NUESTRA HABITACIÓN EN LA MADRUGADA…”

This is the single most damaging thing on the page — a conversion-killer sitting in the social-proof section. The design calls for three short, curated quotes about the booking experience (“booked online on a Sunday night”, “planned everything from Chicago”, “secured the venue with 10%”), each with a 5-star row, couple names, venue and year.

Replace with the curated quotes from the design (the support team has these stories in WhatsApp, per the design's note); add the star row; never pipe raw venue reviews into this section.

Blocker

Hero trust line is cut off mid-sentence

Reads “+1,000 group weddings · 24 bookings this month · Payment protected with” — the word Stripe is missing. A truncated trust claim reads as broken exactly where credibility matters most.

“Payment protected with Stripe”, matching the design and the footer.

Should fix

Venue cards lost their price framing and context line

Design cards read: type + distance (“Hacienda · Huaxtla, 30 min from GDL · View map”), “From $35,500 MXN” with an “up to -50%” badge, and the footnote “venue only · final price depends on your date”. Live cards show a bare “$ 245,000 MXN” with no “From”, no badge, only “Zapopan, Jalisco”, and the footnote mistranslated as “Venue only or final package depending on your date” — which changes the meaning from “price varies by date” to “what you get varies”.

Restore “From” + discount badge + type/distance line; footnote: “venue only · final price depends on your date”.

Should fix

Zero-star ratings on display

Antiguo Colegio de San Diego and Los Hilos show “★ [0]” on Home and Results. The design never shows a rating without reviews (always “4.9 (23)”-style, rating with count).

Hide the rating element until a venue has reviews; when shown, pair it with the review count.

Should fix

Instagram grid: two duplicate photos, two near-blank frames

The design shows four distinct venue photos. Live shows the same couple photo twice plus two washed-out, almost-white frames that read as broken images. The follower count (28.6k) is correctly bound — good.

Four distinct venue/wedding photos per design.

Polish

Occupied state leaks into the home carousel

The fourth card (Finca by Alofa) appears grayed with “Occupied on your date”. In the design, occupied treatment belongs to Results; the home carousel is inspirational and shows every venue at its best. Also: the location default reads “3 cities” where the design says “Guadalajara and nearby”.

Home carousel always shows the standard card; label the default region per design.

2Results

Should fix

Cards dropped guest capacity and review counts

Design cards carry “up to [350] guests” and the rating with its count (“4.9 (23)”) — two of the three facts a couple filters venues by. Live cards show only city + rating number.

Add capacity (“up to N guests”) to the card meta line and review count next to the rating.

Polish

Results keeps the full home header

The design gives Results a slimmer header — logo with “VENUES · MÉXICO” wordmark, “Explore venues” underlined as active, no category tab bar. Live reuses the entire home header including the “Venue only / Concept venues / Beyond the venue” tabs.

Optional: adopt the slim results header; if the tabs stay, mark the active state consistently.

Polish

“Voguex” watermark visible on occupied-card photos

Grayscale occupied cards show a faint “Voguex” watermark overlay not present in the design. Verify it's an intentional image policy and not a placeholder asset.

Confirm intent; if watermarking stays, keep it subtle and consistent across all photos, not only occupied ones.

3Venue page

Blocker

The page contradicts itself on what the rental includes

“Your venue rental includes” lists Valet parking ✓. Thirty lines later, “RENTAL DOES NOT INCLUDE” lists Valet parking services. On a page that takes money, a contradiction about what's included is a refund dispute waiting to happen.

Make the checklist the single source of truth per venue; reconcile valet parking (and re-verify every item) against the venue contract.

Should fix

“The venue” is a raw content dump, not the curated description

The design calls for a three-line curated description. Live pastes the vendor's full sheet: “RENTAL INCLUDES / RENTAL DOES NOT INCLUDE” bullet lists that duplicate (and contradict) the checklist module above, plus unstructured contract details — $25,000 MXN security deposit, overtime until 3:00 AM, two setup days. There's also a typo: “Basic lightning in the rectangular ballrooms” → lighting.

Trim the prose to the curated description; move deposit/overtime/setup into the structured includes module or a “Good to know” block; fix the typo.

Should fix

Title row stripped of its key facts

Design: “★ 4.9 · 12 reviews | Huaxtla, Jalisco · 30 min from Guadalajara | Up to [300] guests | Own chapel”. Live shows only “★ 4.9 Zona Centro Guadalajara, Jalisco” — no review count, no capacity, no signature feature.

Restore the full fact row: rating · review count | location · distance | capacity | signature feature.

Should fix

Gallery layout broken and “See all photos” missing

Design: one hero image + four thumbnails in a tight grid, with a “See all 11 photos” button. Live: four images with a visible empty gap in the grid and no way to see the rest of the gallery.

1+4 grid per design (or 1+3 adjusted gracefully when only 4 photos exist) and the “See all N photos” button.

Should fix

“Couples who married here” section missing

The design includes per-venue couple cards (photo, names, wedding date, guest count, quote). The live page jumps from the description straight to the trust chips.

Add the section; the design notes the support team already has these stories in WhatsApp. If content isn't ready at launch, hide the section rather than shipping placeholders.

Should fix

“Also available on your date” shows gray placeholder tiles

The related-venue cards (EntreBosque, Los Hilos) render as flat gray boxes with a “Voguex” wordmark instead of venue photos.

Bind the venues' real cover photos.

Polish

Price-card copy ignores that a date is already chosen

With “Saturday, 14 November 2026” selected and an exact price shown, the caption still reads “exact price appears when you pick a date”. The design pairs that caption with the no-date state (“From $35,500 · up to -50%”).

State-dependent copy: no date → “From” price + discount badge + that caption; date chosen → exact price, caption dropped.

4Hold checkout

Should fix

Payment timeline dropped the signing amount

Design: “At signing · 20% also on Voguex — $40,000” (computed for the venue). Live: “At signing · 20% also on Voguex” with no amount — for this venue it should say — $26,000. The whole point of this module is “no surprises”; showing the real number is the promise.

Compute and display the 20% amount on the third bullet, as the first bullet already does for the 10%.

Should fix

International box lost the USD estimate

Design ends the “Planning your wedding from outside Mexico?” card with “≈ $[X,XXX] USD for this date, tax included”. Live cuts that line, which is the one international couples actually need.

Add the approximate USD conversion for the selected date (clearly marked as approximate).

5What already matches

So the list above lands in context — the build gets the hard parts right:

  • Full page structure and section order on all four screens
  • Header, category tabs and “coming soon” badges on Home
  • Three-step “How it works” with correct 10% / 20% / 30% logic
  • Results grid: Available / Occupied states, grayscale treatment, “See nearby available dates” link
  • “None of these worked?” tip card with ±3 days / ±1 week / full month chips
  • Price reassurance line under results (“no fine print · 10% hold · 8-day 100% refund”)
  • Hold flow: Date → Hold → Sign stepper, payment timeline, summary card, Stripe notes
  • Magic-link account copy, Edna advisor note, 30-min hold-while-you-ask
  • Green cancellation reassurance on the venue page
  • Footer content, ES | EN toggle, Stripe footer line
